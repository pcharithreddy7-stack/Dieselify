FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What areas do you serve?

Dieselify serves customers all across Bangalore. Whether your site is a construction project, hospital, data centre, corporate facility, campus, factory, farm, or another commercial operation, our team can arrange on-site diesel delivery within the city.

2. Who can order diesel from Dieselify?

Dieselify supports commercial, industrial, institutional, and operational fuel requirements. Businesses running generators, heavy machinery, backup power systems, production facilities, campuses, healthcare sites, agricultural equipment, and similar operations can request a delivery tailored to their site and fuel volume.

3. How do I request a diesel delivery on Dieselify?

Call our team or submit the quote form with your delivery location, required diesel quantity, preferred delivery date, and any site-access details. We confirm the order, plan the route, share an ETA, and arrange a certified tanker for on-site delivery. You can also use the Get a Quote button anywhere on the website to start your request.

4. Is your diesel safe and compliant?

Yes. Dieselify follows safe and compliant fuel-handling practices for every delivery. We are PESO Certified, DPIIT Recognized, an Authorized Fuel Partner, and GST Registered. Our certified delivery process is designed to provide the exact ordered quantity safely at your site, with a digital invoice after delivery.
